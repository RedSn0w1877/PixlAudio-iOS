# Song Drop and the tap fixes, iOS (2026-11-21)

Branch `song-drop` (cut from `song-tap`, not merged). iOS port of Android's branch `song-drop` (its `handoff/2026-11-21-song-drop.md`, the main
section and the Follow-up, spec 5 then spec 6). The pure core is at **spec version 6**. Nothing here has run on a phone: it is verified by the
PixlCore tests on this machine and by CI (see the end).

The iPhone is the reference signal scale (Android is normalised to it: -15 dB on a Pixel). So on iOS the offset is 0, the readings go to the
core as they are, and NORMAL is the iPhone's own tuning, unchanged.

## What the owner asked for, and where it is

| Ask | Where |
|---|---|
| "the first haptic tap was a good point to send data at" | prefetch and commit: the receiver holds the checked offer before the tap, the tap is the commit (`SongTapSession`, `SongTapBleRoute`) |
| "it can find phones too easily" | the near gate (-70/-78) decides connecting, serving and firing; the list gate (-85/-92) only decides who is listed; the sender serves only the armed phone |
| names, "Song drop to X?" Yes / No, a Song Drop button next to Share, "tap phones together to share" | `SongTapNames`, `SongTapNearbyBook`, Settings > Song Tap > Your name, `SongDrop.start`, the nearby list, the prompt, the armed card |
| "when 1 phone does the initial haptic tap it signals the other" | the one-sided tap: event `0x04` and Control `04 aa aa aa aa`, `SongTapPeerTap.judge` |
| "overall just make it a lot more sensitive" | `SongTapSensitivity.high` is the default; Normal is the old iPhone tuning |

## The core (`Packages/PixlCore/Sources/PixlLibrary/SongTap/`)

- `SongTapSensitivity` (`normal`, `high`, default `high`): baselineShift 3, fastShift 1, freezeDb 6 in both; jump 15 / 9 dB, hold 150 / 60 ms,
  dwell 400 / 200 ms, impulse window 400, jerk 1800 / 900 milli g (optional, never required), cooldown 2000 / 1200 ms. `SongTapDetector.step` and
  `SongTapImpulse.step` take the profile.
- `SongTapTuning`: near -70/-78, list -85/-92, hold -45 dBm for 250 ms (`FireReason.hold`), released below -55 (`closeReleaseDbm`), dwell minimum -58,
  glow -80...-45. `closeSinceMs` / `closeUsed` in the detector state; the golden step line is unchanged.
- `SongTapNames`: 1 to 20 Unicode scalars, advertisement name at most 24 UTF-8 bytes at a scalar boundary, never Swift `Character`s.
- `SongTapNearbyBook`: `update`, `remove`, `clear`, `ignore`, `isIgnored`, `visible`, `promptFor`; stale 4 s, same-phone gap 1.5 s, ignore 60 s.
- `SongTapPeerTap.judge`: followed, said yes, own smoothed signal at least -70, 1200 ms since the last own fire or peer tap, in that order.
- `SongTapHandshake`: `GO_DELAY_MS` = 0; no `go`, no read, no ack before the beam.
- `SongTapSession`: `Approve`, `Republish`, `OfferRead`, `OfferReadLost`, `OfferPrefetched`, `OfferGone`, `TapDetected(peer, videoId, nowMs)`, the
  `target` / `offerRead` / `tapSeen` / `acked` / `beamDone` / `pre` state, the `target` timeout (60 s). A tap with the offer in hand does
  `haptic(tick)`, `saveSong`, `scheduleTimeout(save)`, `startBeam(receive, now)` in one reduction.
- `SongTapWire`: Name characteristic `b7f3a1c0-5d2e-4c9a-8e61-3f4d7a2b9c15` (version byte `01` then the clean name in UTF-8), Event `02` offer ready,
  `03` offer withdrawn, `04` tap (exactly one byte), Control `04 aa aa aa aa`; `encodeAdvertName` / `decodeAdvertName` for the company id `0xFFFF` block.

`SongTapFlow/`: `SongTapBleRoute` (the route: strict gate for connecting, serving and firing; prefetch with a 2 s retry and a 20 s refresh; names;
nearby list and prompt from advertisements only; the pinned target; the one-sided tap both ways with no echo), `SongTapEngine` (approve, republish,
cancel drop, sensitivity, drop mode, the prefetched payload, `keepBluetooth` while the sender's beam runs), `SongTapDiagnostics`.

## The app

- `SongTapBluetoothRadio`: advertises the role's service UUID and the short name as the **local name**; reads Android's company-id `0xFFFF`
  manufacturer block from `CBAdvertisementDataManufacturerDataKey` (else the local name) through `SongTapWire.advertisedName`; the Name characteristic
  on the sender's server and `readName` on the client; `notify(client:value:)` replaces the old go notification.
- `SongTapController`: `approve`, `decideNo`, `cancelDrop`, the 20 s target refresh, `beginSongDrop` / `endSongDrop` (the picked song is offered until the
  screen is left, not until the app goes inactive), `setName`, `setSensitivity` (also reaches the accelerometer threshold), `ble.onNearbyChange`.
- `SongTapSettings`: `song_tap_your_name` (default `UIDevice.name`, "iPhone" when empty after cleaning), `song_tap_sensitivity` (default High).
- Screens: Song Tap > Send shows "Phones nearby" (Close / Nearby), the "Song drop to X?" alert (Yes / No; No ignores that phone for 60 s), then the armed
  card "Tap phones together to share" / "Sharing with X" / Cancel; "From X" on the receiver's card and beam card; Settings > Song Tap: Your name, Sensitivity;
  the diagnostics screen shows the gates, the active gate, the profile and thresholds, the hold level, the offset 0 note, offer in hand, names and the peer-tap verdict.
- Song Drop entry: a round tertiary glass button right of Share in the song options sheet (`SongOptionsSheet`), "Song Drop" in the player's ⋯ menu, and in the
  long-press quick actions. All call `SongDrop.start(song, env:, router:)`: the song is what is offered, a sheet that is up goes away, the Song Tap cover opens on Send. With Song Tap
  off the screen shows "Turn on Song Tap". The words are Android's `strings_songtap.xml`; none of the banned words are used.

## Deviations from Android

1. **Offset 0, no Calibrate.** The iPhone is the reference. The diagnostics screen says so (offset 0 dB, normalised = raw).
2. **Advertising power cannot be chosen on iOS.** Android advertises at LOW (-15 dBm); an iPhone advertises at a fixed power. The near gate is the only thing that keeps
   a far phone from being connected to, and the two phones read each other differently (a Pixel reads about 15 dB hotter than an iPhone at the same pose, which is what Android's offset removes on its side), so
   the gate may need a per-pair look with the diagnostics screen.
3. **Short name in the local name.** iOS puts a name that does not fit the 31 byte advertisement in the scan response or shortens it; a short name is all the list needs, the full
   name comes from the Name characteristic after connecting.
4. **No player collapse.** Android collapses the player sheet before opening the screen; iOS opens the Song Tap cover over the player, as the ⋯ menu's Song Tap row already does.
5. **The ring code goldens** (`ring-*.jsonl`) are in Android's golden folder and its manifest, so they are copied byte for byte and their hashes are checked, but no ring code is ported here.
6. **A lone surrogate in `names.jsonl`** (`isValid` of `"\ud800 lone"`): a Swift `String` cannot hold one (the JSON parser turns it into U+FFFD, which is itself a clean character),
   so the test decides that one case from the raw JSON and expects `false` as Android does. Every `clean` / `advertName` / `fromAdvert` case of it passes unchanged.
7. **HIGH on the iPhone's own trace fires on the approach as well** (a 9 dB rise from -64 held 200 ms) and again at the touch after the 1.2 s cooldown. Android's tests do not assert a count for
   that pairing; `SongTapRealTraceTests` asserts no fire at rest, no fire later than NORMAL's, and the cooldown.
8. **The Song Drop button is a capsule** (the glass kit has no round-on-capsule mix) in the options row; the Android button is round.

## How to test with two phones

Two phones with this build (or this and the Pixel build of Android's `song-drop`), Bluetooth on, both with Song Tap allowed, a song that can be sent on the sender.

1. Settings > Song Tap > Your name on both: two different names. Developer mode on: Settings > Song Tap > Song Tap diagnostics.
2. Receiver: Song Tap > Receive. Sender: play a song, open Song Tap > Send (or: a song's options > the Song Drop button right of Share, or the ⋯ menu's Song Drop).
3. Sender: "Phones nearby" lists the receiver by name from a couple of metres ("Nearby"). Receiver's diagnostics: gate now = list only, may connect and beam = no, connection = none.
4. Bring them close: at the near gate the sender asks "Song drop to X?" and the row says "Close"; the receiver connects, reads the name, and after Yes shows "offer in hand: yes". Try No: that phone is not asked again for a minute.
5. Yes: "Tap phones together to share". Touch gently and hold (no bump): one tick on each phone at the same moment, both beams, the receiver's card says "From <sender>", "Adding", "Added to your library" with Undo. The first time it asks Add / Not now.
6. One-sided: move only one phone towards the other while the second rests beside it: both should still tick together (the log lines "the sender reports a tap: accept" / "tap told to the receiver").
7. Rest the phones 20 to 30 cm apart for a minute: nothing may fire. Try Normal and High (Settings > Song Tap > Sensitivity): High should react to a lighter touch.
8. Range: walk apart while armed: nothing connects beyond the near gate; the list drops the phone a few seconds after the list gate. Use the Mark buttons and Copy log and send the numbers back.
9. Cross platform in both directions (iPhone sender with a Pixel receiver, and the other way): names show on both, the one-sided tap works from either side.
