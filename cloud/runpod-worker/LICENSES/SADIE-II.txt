SADIE II Database (head-related impulse responses)
==================================================

What is used
------------
PixlAudio's Spatial audio is computed with head-related impulse responses of the Neumann KU100 dummy head taken
from the SADIE II Database, subject D1: D1_48K_24bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa (8802 directions, 256 taps, 48 kHz),
from D1_HRIR_SOFA.zip.

    SADIE II Database, version 2-2
    AudioLab and Gavin Kearney, University of York
    https://zenodo.org/records/12092466   doi:10.5281/zenodo.12092466

Changes (Apache License 2.0, section 4(b))
------------------------------------------
The file distributed here, src/pixl_worker/spatial/data/hrir_ku100.npz, is a modified derivative of the
database, made on 2026-11-10 by cloud/runpod-worker/tools/build_hrir.py in
https://github.com/RedSn0w1877/PixlAudio-iOS:

1. about 900 of the measured directions are kept: the one nearest to each point of a regular grid (every 5
   degrees of azimuth on the rings from -45 to +60 degrees of elevation, coarser rings near the poles);
2. the set is made exactly left-right symmetric: the right half of the sphere is the mirror image of the left
   half, and on the median plane both ears get the average;
3. the common leading silence is removed (the same number of samples from every response) and the tails are
   faded out;
4. the set is diffuse-field equalised (a minimum-phase correction limited to +/- 12 dB);
5. the samples are stored as 16-bit integers with a scale factor.

Two more files are computed from it and shipped in the PixlAudio apps (iOS and Android):
schema/v1/spatial/spatial_preview_v1.json and schema/v1/spatial/spatial_preview_golden_v1.json. They hold short
filters and sparse reflection patterns derived from a few of those responses, not the responses themselves.
