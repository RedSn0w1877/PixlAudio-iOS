# Logo: concept C "Glyph" (2026-10-07, branch `wt/logo`)

Hoa picked logo concept C as the universal PixlAudio logo: the Android icon's monochrome silhouette as a white
frosted-glass glyph on a sky-to-violet gradient. Design notes: `docs/design.md` › Brand mark. New APIs:
`docs/api-notes.md` › Logo and launch screen.

## What changed
- `ci/make-icon.py` is the single source of the logo (concept C by default; A and B stay for `--compare`). It writes
  the app icon (Any, Dark, Tinted), `BrandMark.imageset` (light/dark tile, 120 pt), `BrandGlyph.imageset` (template
  SVG traced from the icon's glyph) and `docs/brand/pixlaudio-icon.png` (README). `--preview sheet.png` renders a
  contact sheet to check before committing.
- About: the hero circle shows `BrandGlyph` (28 pt in the 48 pt `primaryContainer` circle), as Android does.
- Launch screen: `BrandMark` on `LaunchBackground` (the brand background), like Android's splash.
- Owner decisions applied: About keeps Android's layout; the welcome page stays without a logo (as on Android);
  Icon Composer `.icon` later.
- Android (`awt/logo` in the Android worktree, from `android-int-oct3`): the same logo as an adaptive icon for the
  phone and Wear apps, plus the in-app glyph and the README icon, rendered by the Android repo's
  `tools/make-launcher-icon.py`, which imports this script.
